Payroll Planner

Plan your salary, track what you spend and watch your savings grow. Everything stays on your phone.

Install it first

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Payroll from your home screen and set it up there.
  1. Tap ⋮ at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
  3. Open Payroll from your home screen.

Open this page on your phone to install it like an app.

On iPhone the installed app keeps its own storage, so anything you enter here in Safari won’t carry over.

Get started

Answer a few questions about your salary and where it goes. It takes about a minute, and you can change everything later.

Your numbers stay on your phone. There’s no account and no server. Payroll Planner helps you plan; it isn’t financial advice.
